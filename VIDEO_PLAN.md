## Story: "The bill that came after"

Ethan did nothing wrong and still got a $43,110 bill. A hospital receptionist, **Dana**, introduces Clearbill to him at the front desk. The same Dana then pitches it to other hospitals at the end. (Dana Lee is also the front-desk login in the demo, so the story and the product match.)

Structure: story (about 1:05), app demo with voice-over (about 1:15), Dana speaks to other hospitals (about 0:30), close (about 0:10).

### Part 2: The app, live, with voice-over (1:05 to 2:20). Screen recording

The voice-over now becomes the narrator, in the same calm voice, and the recording shows the product working.

1. **Patient app on a phone (about 40 s).** Sign in as Ethan (123 456 789). Open the $43,110 bill. AI explains each line and flags the likely mistakes. Set a budget of $250. The patient's agent and the clinic's agent talk. Deal: $250 a month for 18 months on a fair share of $4,500. Approve on PayPal's sandbox page. "Your plan is active."
2. **Clinic site, billing admin (about 30 s).** The flagged bill, the agent conversation and the plan appear on the clinic side. Then the Billing desk with the live PayPal subscription.
3. **Safeguards (about 5 s).** Front desk sees no amounts, names are masked, the audit log records who did what.

### Part 3: Dana speaks to other hospitals (2:20 to 2:50)

Dana, in the same hospital office, speaks to the camera: "If your clinic is chasing unpaid bills, this is the other half." Over her voice, the screen shows the clinic's **AG Studio dashboard** (Money, Patients and requests, Care and activity), the **Billing desk** with EMI plans, and the patient list with the audit log. Key lines: "Fewer unpaid balances. Clear plans. PayPal collects. Your staff sees only what they need."

### Part 4: Close (2:50 to 3:00)

Text on screen: "Rules decide the money. AI explains. PayPal collects." Then the live URL: https://clearbill-sm7c.onrender.com and "Clearbill: understand your bill, agree a plan, pay with PayPal."
